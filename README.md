# Spanish New World Explorer

## English

Spanish New World Explorer is a free educational and historical project that recreates the voyages of Spanish explorers across the Atlantic Ocean and the New World.

Visitors will guide a Spanish sailing ship through a 3D environment and discover islands, coastlines, and territories connected to Spanish exploration.

At every destination, an interactive information window will provide:

- Modern country information.
- National flag.
- National coat of arms.
- Etymology of the country's name.
- Vexillology and flag symbolism.
- Name of the explorer.
- Explorer life years.
- Year of discovery.
- Circumstances of the discovery.
- Indigenous peoples and civilizations.
- Colonial history.
- Independence and modern history.

The goal of the project is to combine history, geography, education, and interactive 3D exploration into a single free learning experience.

## Project Vision

The goal of this project is to create a free, browser-based historical simulator in which a Spanish sailing ship travels across the Atlantic Ocean and reaches islands, coasts, and territories connected to Spanish exploration and conquest in the New World.

When the ship reaches a destination, an interactive information window opens and presents detailed historical and cultural information about the modern country and the Spanish encounter with that region.
