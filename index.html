Interactive Historical Simulator

Explore the Spanish Voyages to the New World

Sail a Spanish ship across the Atlantic, discover historical destinations, and open rich information cards about flags, national emblems, etymology, explorers, Indigenous peoples, and colonial history.

Project Overview

This project will become a free browser-based 3D educational experience. The first version begins as a clean static prototype and will later grow into a full Three.js sailing simulator.
